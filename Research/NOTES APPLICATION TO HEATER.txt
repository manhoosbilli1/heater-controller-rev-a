Avoiding thermal runaway:
usually seen in applications powered by constant current source whereas the battery is a constant voltage source, as the temperature rises the resistance rises, as resistance rises Temperature of the trace rises, because current is constant more heat is generated which further increases the resistance and hence a condition is reached where the traces burn or explode: a thermal runaway
